Local basics

Currency, voltage, transport, emergency numbers and other things to know before you go.

Currency
Japanese yen (¥). Some small shops and rural areas are cash-only
Voltage & plugs
100 V / 50–60 Hz, type A plugs (Taiwanese appliances usually work as-is)
Tipping
Not expected (service is normally included)
Emergency
Police 110 / Fire & ambulance 119
Transport
Suica, PASMO, ICOCA and other transit IC cards work on most trains and buses nationwide
Tap water
Safe to drink
Language
Japanese; major stations and sights often have English/Chinese signs
Entry rules
Online pre-registration (e.g. Visit Japan Web) and tax-free rules change; check official sources
